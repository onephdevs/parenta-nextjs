# Job: Record a building expense

**Replaces:** A cash-out row in Excel (salary, repair, fuel, cleaning).

**Who does this:** Office admin / Owner

**When:** You paid a vendor or staff and need it on the month’s cash-flow.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Bills & Expenses** → **Expenses** (`/admin/financial/expenses`). Click **Record Expense**.
2. **Amount:** enter **Amount**. **Category:** choose **Category** and **Description**. **Date:** set **Expense Date**.
3. Optional: **Building**, **Unit / Room**, **Vendor**. Click **Record Expense**.

## Done when
You land on the expense detail. After refresh it appears on **Expenses**. It will count in **Disbursement / Cash-flow**.

## Watch out for
**Amount** must be greater than 0. Pick a **Category** that matches the spend (Staff salary, Food allowance, Fuel / diesel, etc.).
