# Job: Export this month’s bills and expenses

**Replaces:** Totalling Meralco, water, and cash-out rows in Excel for the owner.

**Who does this:** Owner / office admin

**When:** Month close, after utility bills and expenses are entered.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Bills & Expenses** → **Reports** (`/admin/bills-expenses/reports`).
2. Set **Report type** (**Summary by category** or **Detail list**), **Period** (**This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Last quarter**, **Last 6 months**, **This year**), **Building** (**All buildings** or one).
3. Click **Generate**. Then **Print**, **PDF**, or **Excel**.

## Also on this page
- Title **Reports** / **Expense & utility bill reporting**. **Back to Bills & Expenses**.
- Empty until **Generate**. Copy: **Choose report type, period, and building, then click Generate.**
- No search of individual bills here — that is **Utility Bills** / **Expenses**.
- Toast **Report Generated**. Export without generate: **Generate the report first**.

## Done when
The table matches the period. After refresh, **Generate** again — this screen does not store a snapshot.

## Watch out for
**Generate** first or **PDF** / **Excel** will not run. Utility splits and expenses must already be recorded.
