# Job: Sign in as staff and work the repair queue

**Replaces:** A caretaker walking units with a paper list of repairs.

**Who does this:** Staff (not an office admin login)

**When:** Maintenance should be done without full office access.

## Steps
1. Open `/staff` (staff sign-in `/auth/staff/signin` goes to the same **Login**).
2. **Staff Portal** → **Operations**: **Maintenance Queue** (`/admin/maintenance`), **Rooms** (`/admin/rooms`), **Activity Logs**.
3. Work tickets as in **Work a repair ticket**.

## Also on this page
- Header **Staff Portal**. Admin note may offer **open Admin Dashboard** if the account is admin.
- No separate staff maintenance UI — it reuses the office screens.
- Not linked from the admin sidebar. Office logins are created on **Users** as admin, not staff.

## Done when
You can open the maintenance queue and save a ticket status. After refresh the status holds.

## Watch out for
This is not a limited “caretaker” office role — caretaker was withdrawn. If they need Payments and Reports, create an office login instead.
