# Job: Renew a tenant’s lease

**Replaces:** Extending the end date on Excel and collecting another deposit/advance by hand.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** The current term is ending and they are staying.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tenants** → open the tenant → **Lease**. Click **View** on the current row, then **Renew**. Or summary **Lease** → **Renew**. URL: `/admin/tenants/[id]/leases/[leaseId]/renew`.
2. Review **Lease Information** / **Renewal Options**: **Retain previous lease details**, **Carry over deposit**, **Waive advance payment**, **Waive any accrued penalties**, **Waive outstanding balance**. Set **Lease Template**, **Rent Amount**, **Start Date**, **End Date**.
3. Click **Renew lease**, then confirm **Renewing this lease will update the tenant's records and apply the selected settings. Proceed?**

## Also on this page
- Heading **Renew Lease** / **Renewing lease for:**
- Sections: **Lease Details**, **Lease Duration**, **Financial Summary** (**Deposit Amount**, **Advance Amount**, **Initial Cashout**, **First Due Date**).
- **Cancel**. Saving state **Renewing…**
- Edit (not renew): **Edit Lease** → **Save changes** → **Confirm Lease Changes** (requires **Reason**).
- Leasing **Renewals** tab lists leases nearing end — it does not renew by itself.

## Done when
After refresh **Lease History** shows the new term. Rent and dates match what you saved.

## Watch out for
**Property, unit, and dates are required.** **Please select a lease template.** Waive options change cash expected — do not tick **Waive outstanding balance** unless you mean to.
