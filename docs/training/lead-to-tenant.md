# Job: Inquiry → occupied unit (leasing pipeline)

**Replaces:** Facebook / walk-in notes, then a later Excel move-in when they actually take the room.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** Someone asks about a vacant unit and you need to track them through viewing, payment, and lease.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tasks** (`/admin/tasks`, board **Onboarding**). Click **Add opportunity**.
2. **Contact:** **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone**. **Property:** **Building**, **Room**, **Monthly rent (₱)**. Save with **Create opportunity**. Drag or **Move to stage…** through **New inquiry** → **Viewing scheduled** → **Viewing done** → **Documents** → **Background check**.
3. Open the card → **Payment**: mark payment received. **Lease:** set **Lease Start Date** and **Lease Template**, then click **Generate lease**. The card moves to **Lease signed**.

## Done when
After refresh, the person is on **Tenants**, the room is occupied, and the card is in **Lease signed**. New contacts get an emailed temporary password (they change it on first portal login).

## Watch out for
**Generate lease** stays off until **Building**, **Room**, and **Payment received** are set. Do this on **Onboarding**, not the Payments board. Optional: **Import** a CSV of opportunities, or use **Advanced Filters** to narrow the board.
