# Job: Send payment reminder emails

**Replaces:** Chasing unpaid rent one by one on Messenger.

**Who does this:** Admin

**When:** After invoices are out and some units are still unpaid.

## Steps
1. Open **Email Reminders** (`/admin/notifications`). There is no sidebar item — Dashboard notifications “view all”, or type the URL.
2. Under **Generate Payment Reminders**, click **Generate Reminders**.
3. Under **Process Notification Queue**, click **Process Queue**.

## Also on this page
- Section **How Notifications Work**.
- Header bell is **not** this page (bell → Settings → Notifications).
- No inbox, no compose, no template editor here.
- Settings **Notifications** tab only toggles In-app / Email categories.

## Done when
Reminders are generated and the queue processes without an error. Tenants who should get email have an address on file.

## Watch out for
This is easy to miss (not in the sidebar). Generating without **Process Queue** leaves mail sitting. Tenants without email will not get a message.
