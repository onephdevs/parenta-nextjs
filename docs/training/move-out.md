# Job: Move-out (unit becomes vacant, history retained)

**Replaces:** Crossing the name off Excel and leaving the unit “empty” in the notebook, while keeping past occupancy.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** The tenant’s last day; the room must be free for the next occupant.

## Steps
1. Optional inspection worksheet: **Leasing** → **Move-outs** → **Start move-out** (also on the lease detail). Pick the occupied lease if needed. This does **not** empty the room.
2. To vacate: Sidebar **Properties** → open the occupied room (`/admin/rooms/[id]`), or **Leasing** → open the lease. Under **Tenant assignment** / **Current Tenant**, click **End Assignment**.
3. In **End Tenant Assignment**, set **End Date**, optional **Notes**, then click **End Assignment**. If a worksheet is open: **Leasing** → **Move-outs** → fill **Actual move-out date** and refund fields → **Finalize move-out & refund**.

## Done when
After refresh the room is vacant and the person still appears in tenant / lease history (not deleted). Toast: **Tenant unassigned successfully!**

## Watch out for
**End Date** is required. **Terminate** only ends the contract on paper (status **Notice given**, planned move-out date). The unit stays occupied until **End Assignment** or **Finalize**.
