# Job: Chase unpaid rent on Tasks

**Replaces:** A paper follow-up list of who still owes this period.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** After invoices are out. Recording the cash is still **Collect rent** / **Confirm a receipt**.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tasks** (`/admin/tasks`). Switch to the **Payments** board (not **Onboarding**).
2. Click **Add rent payment** if a card is missing. Fill **First name**, **Last name**, **Building**, **Unit / room**, **Rent due**, **Amount (₱)**. Click **Create rent follow-up**.
3. Drag through **Upcoming** → **Due** → **Reminder sent** → **Overdue** → **Paid** (or **Refund** / **Escalation**).

## Also on this page
- Same chrome as Onboarding: search, **Advanced Filters**, **Import**, **Bulk Actions**, **Configure stages**.
- **Rent due date is required.**
- **Building expenses** board: **Add bill or expense** — vendor follow-up, not the Expenses ledger.
- **Sync pipelines** can refresh cards from leases/invoices/maintenance.
- Confirming money: **Payments** hub, not this board.

## Done when
After refresh the card is in **Paid** when the invoice is collected. Sync if the board looks stale.

## Watch out for
Do this on **Payments**, not **Onboarding**. Moving a card to **Paid** is follow-up — it does not post a receipt by itself.
