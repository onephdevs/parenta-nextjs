# Job: Check who paid this month (unit × month)

**Replaces:** The Excel grid of units down the side and months across the top (paid / partial / blank).

**Who does this:** Office admin / Owner

**When:** During the month to chase unpaid rooms, or before closing the period.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Reports** (`/admin/reports`). Open **Unit × Month Collections** (`/admin/reports/unit-month`).
2. Set **Start month**, **End month**, and optional **Property** (**All properties** or one building).
3. Click **Generate matrix**. Green **Paid**, amber **Partial**, red **Unpaid**, grey **Vacant**.

## Done when
The grid matches the rooms you expect. Unpaid cells are the follow-up list. After refresh, generate again — this screen does not save a snapshot.

## Watch out for
**Start month** and **End month** are required. Admin units are excluded (revenue units only). This is a check, not recording a payment — use **Collect rent** or **Confirm a receipt** to change a cell.
