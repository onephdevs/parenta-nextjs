# Job: Give notice (terminate on paper)

**Replaces:** Writing a move-out date in the notebook while the tenant still lives there.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** They gave notice (or you ended the term) but they have not left yet. The room must stay occupied until move-out day.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tenants** → open the tenant. Summary **Lease** → **Terminate**. Or **Leasing** → open the lease → **Terminate**. (Profile link uses `?action=terminate`.)
2. In **Terminate lease**, set **Planned move-out date**. Optional: **Reason (optional)**.
3. Click **Terminate**.

## Also on this page
- Dialog copy: **End {name}'s contract on paper. This does not empty the unit.**
- Success: **Lease ended on paper** / **The unit stays occupied until End Assignment or Finalize move-out.**
- Leasing list status **Notice given** — **ended on paper, still occupying**. Filter **Notice given**.
- This is not **End Assignment** and not **Finalize move-out** (those vacate — see **Move-out**).
- No search on the dialog.

## Done when
After refresh the lease shows **Notice given**. The room is still occupied. **Planned move-out date** is saved.

## Watch out for
**Move-out date required.** If you meant to free the room today, do **End Assignment** instead — **Terminate** will leave them in the unit.
