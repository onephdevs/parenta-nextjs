# Job: Issue this month’s rent invoices

**Replaces:** Writing a rent row in Excel so there is something to tick when money arrives.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** Start of the billing period, before you collect rent. One invoice per tenant you expect to pay.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Payments** → **Invoices** (`/admin/financial/invoices`). Click **Create Invoice**.
2. **Tenant & Room:** choose **Tenant**, then **Room**. **Billing Period:** set **Due Date** (required). Optional: **Billing Period Start** and **Billing Period End**.
3. **Invoice Items:** fill **Description**, **Type** (**Rent**, **Utilities**, **Fees**, **Deposit**, or **Other**), **Quantity**, and **Unit Price**. Click **Create Invoice**.

## Done when
The invoice list shows the new row after refresh. **Collect rent** can now attach a payment to it.

## Watch out for
**Tenant**, **Room**, **Due Date**, **Description**, and **Unit Price** are required. There is no sidebar button to bill every tenant at once.
