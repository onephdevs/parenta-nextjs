# Job: Read this month’s money at a glance

**Replaces:** Scanning Excel totals without a chart.

**Who does this:** Owner / office admin

**When:** A quick look at revenue, occupancy, and upcoming dues — not for recording cash.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Payments** → **Financial Dashboard** (`/admin/financial/dashboard`).
2. Read the KPI cards and charts (revenue, occupancy, upcoming dues).
3. Follow a chart or list into **Payments** / **Invoices** if a number looks wrong.

## Also on this page
- Type `/admin/financial` for the financial hub (Payments, Invoices, Expenses, Late fees, gateways) — not in the sidebar.
- **Reports & Analytics** is a different page (`/admin/financial/reports`).
- **Advanced Financial Analytics** (`/admin/financial/advanced-analytics`) — deeper benchmarks.
- No Process Payment button required on this dashboard — recording cash is **Collect rent**.
- No export on the dashboard itself.

## Done when
You can say whether collections and occupancy look normal for the period. Refresh still shows the same KPIs.

## Watch out for
Charts lag the last payment you just entered until you refresh. This screen does not confirm GCash receipts.
