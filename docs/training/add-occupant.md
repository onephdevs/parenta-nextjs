# Job: Add a roommate / occupant

**Replaces:** A handwritten extra name on the Excel tenant row (spouse, child, relative — not the lease holder).

**Who does this:** Office admin (tenant can also add occupants in the portal)

**When:** Someone else lives in the unit besides the person on the lease.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tenants** → open the tenant → **Profile**. Under **Occupants**, click **Add Occupant** (opens the occupied room). Or: **Properties** → open that room.
2. Under **Current Tenant**, click **Add Occupant**.
3. Fill **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Move-in Date**. Click **Add Occupant**.

## Also on this page
**Add Occupant modal**
- Copy: **Add a non-tenant occupant (e.g., relative, spouse, child) to this room.**
- Optional: **Relationship to Tenant** (**Spouse**, **Child**, **Parent**, **Sibling**, **Relative**, **Friend**, **Other**), **Date of Birth**, **Phone**, **Email**.
- **Emergency Contact (Optional):** **Contact Name**, **Contact Phone**, **Relationship**.
- **Notes**. **Cancel**.
- Toast: **Occupant added successfully!**

**Room**
- Card **Other Occupants**. **Tenancy History** is lease history, not occupant history.

**Tenant Profile Occupants table**
- Columns: **Full Name**, **Contact Details**, **Address**, **Relationship to Tenant**, **Actions**.
- Empty: **No occupants on file. The primary tenant is listed on the lease.**
- Occupant ⋯ actions on the tenant list do not edit yet — change occupants from the room.

**Tenant portal**
- **Profile** → **Occupants**: **Add Occupant** / **Update Occupant**. Required: first name, last name, move-in date.

No search/filter on the occupant list.

## Done when
Toast **Occupant added successfully!** After refresh the name is on the room and on the tenant **Occupants** list.

## Watch out for
The unit must already have a current tenant. Profile **Add Occupant** only jumps to the room — the form is on the room.
