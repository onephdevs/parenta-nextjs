# Job: Open the office home and clear the day’s work

**Replaces:** A paper sticky + scanning Excel to see who still owes and which rooms are empty.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** Start of the day, or whenever you sit down at the desk.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Dashboard** (`/admin`). Read the greeting and the **Quick links** / needs-attention cards (overdue, inquiries, tickets).
2. Use the right rail (**Dashboard actions**): **Record Payment**, **Add note**, **Add Tenant**, **Add Room**, **Maintenance**.
3. Open a card or **Recents** item to finish that task. Optional: **Manage widgets** to show/hide **Quick links**, **Portfolio ledger**, **Recents**, **Your stickies**.

## Also on this page
- **Add quick link** — presets **Properties**, **Tenants**, **Invoices**, **All tasks**.
- **Portfolio ledger** — occupancy / collections snapshot; **Needs attention** block.
- **Your stickies** — personal notes on the home screen.
- **Recents** — last records you opened.
- Header **Search** (properties, tenants, rooms, assets, invoices, documents, leases, payments, expenses). Notification bell. Gear → Settings. **Sign Out**.
- No search bar on the home widgets themselves. No export/print of the dashboard.
- This is not **Financial Dashboard** (`/admin/financial/dashboard`) — that is charts under Payments.

## Done when
You can name what still needs attention today, and a rail action (payment / tenant / room / ticket) opens the matching form.

## Watch out for
The rail **Add Tenant** badge is vacant-room count, not a to-do list. Clearing a card means doing the job on that screen, then refreshing Dashboard.
