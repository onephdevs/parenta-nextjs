# Job: Close the month (disbursement / grand total)

**Replaces:** The Excel waterfall: collections minus expenses, then cash, deposits, and cheques to a grand total.

**Who does this:** Owner / office admin

**When:** After collections and expenses for the period are in (same window as the old ledger, e.g. 16th–15th).

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Reports** → **Disbursement / Cash-flow** (`/admin/reports/disbursement`).
2. Set **From**, **To**, and optional **Property**. Click **Generate**.
3. Read **Total Collection − Expenses = Cash Allowance + Deposit cash + Cheques**. Optional: **Print**. Optional: **Lock period to lifetime** only when that period must not be counted again.

## Also on this page
- Back: **All reports**.
- **Date range** filters. No search of rows. No pagination of the waterfall.
- **Print** is on this page. No Excel/PDF export button here.
- **Lock period to lifetime** — cannot be undone from this screen (**Period locked** / **Already locked**).
- Related: **Collected Amount Report**, **Portfolio Rollup**, **Deposit Report**, **Expense Report** from the Reports hub.

## Done when
The waterfall numbers match what you expect for the dates. After refresh, click **Generate** again with the same dates to confirm.

## Watch out for
**From** and **To** are required. Record expenses first or the expense line stays low. **Lock period to lifetime** is permanent from this screen.
