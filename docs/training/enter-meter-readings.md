# Job: Enter this month’s meter readings

**Replaces:** A paper log of electric/water meter numbers at each building.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** After you walk the meters, before a usage-based split.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Utilities** → **Meter Readings** (`/admin/utilities/readings`). Click **Add Reading** (or **Add Meter Reading**).
2. **Building**, **Utility Type**, **Reading Date**, **Reading Value**. Optional: **Meter Number (optional)**, **Notes (optional)**.
3. Click **Save Reading**.

## Also on this page
- Cards: **Total Readings**, **Unique Meters**, **This Month**, **Buildings**.
- **Search** — **Building, meter, utility...**
- Filters: **Building** (**All Buildings**); **Utility** (**All Types**, **Electricity**, **Water**, **Gas**, **Internet**, **Other**).
- **Cancel** on the form.
- No edit/delete of a reading. No sort / pagination / bulk / export / print.
- Empty: **Get started by adding your first meter reading.**
- Older **Utilities Management** at `/utilities` (from Reports → **Utility Bills Summary**) is a different dashboard.

## Done when
After refresh the reading is in the table. **Cost Allocation** can use it for usage-based split.

## Watch out for
**Building** is required. There is no **Edit** — enter a new reading if you typed the wrong value.
