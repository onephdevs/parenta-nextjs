# Job: Log furniture and appliances in a unit

**Replaces:** A paper inventory of beds, ACs, and appliances per room.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** You buy or move an asset, or need a QR for on-site checks.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Assets** (`/admin/assets`). Click **Add Asset**.
2. Fill **Asset Name**, **Asset Type**. Optional: **Brand**, **Model**, **Serial Number**, **Building**, **Status**, **Condition**, **Purchase Price**, **Current Value**, **Rental Rate (per month)**, **Description**. Click **Save Asset**.
3. Optional: tab **QR Codes** to print a code. Scan later at `/track/asset/[id]`.

## Also on this page
- Header **Export**, **Add Asset**.
- Tabs: **Asset Overview**, **Maintenance Schedule**, **Analytics**, **QR Codes**.
- **Search** — **Name, brand, serial...**
- Filters: **Building**; **Status** (**All Status**, **Available**, **Assigned**, **Maintenance**, **Disposed**); **Condition** (**All Conditions**, **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Poor**, **Damaged**).
- Delete confirms before remove.
- Public scan page is outside admin. `?scan=true` writes a scan note.
- Reports hub **Asset Inventory** / **Asset Assignment Report** open this module.

## Done when
After refresh the asset is in **Asset Overview**. **Export** can download the list. Edit later with **Update Asset**.

## Watch out for
**Asset Name** and **Asset Type** are required. The add form has **Building**, not a room picker. **Disposed** is not the same as delete.
