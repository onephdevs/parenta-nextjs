# Job: Hold a unit (reservation)

**Replaces:** A verbal “hold” on a vacant room plus a deposit screenshot, before a full lease.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** Someone paid a reservation deposit and you need the room held until they sign.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Tenants** → **Reservations** (`/admin/tenants/reservations`), or **Reservations** on the Tenants page. Click **Create Reservation**.
2. **Room**, **Tenant** (or **New Tenant**), **Reservation Date**, **Expiry Date**, **Monthly Rate**, **Reservation Deposit**. Optional: **Advance Payment**, **Utility Deposit**, **Notes**. Click **Create Reservation**.
3. When they take the unit: open the row → **Convert** / **Convert to Assignment**. Set **Lease Start Date** and **Lease Template**. Click **Convert to Assignment**.

## Also on this page
- Cards: **Active Reservations**, **Expired**, **Expiring Soon**, **Converted**.
- **Search** — **Search by tenant, room, or building...**
- **Status:** **All Statuses**, **Active**, **Converted**, **Expired**, **Cancelled**.
- **Sort:** **Sort by Expiry Date**, **Reservation Date**, **Tenant**, **Room**, **Monthly Rate**. Button **Sort**.
- Row: **Convert**, **Cancel**. Detail: **Convert to Assignment**, **Cancel Reservation**, **Back to List**.
- Convert extra: **Lease End Date (Optional)**, deposits, **Automatically generate invoices for the lease period**, **Notes**.
- Cancel confirm: **Cancel reservation?** — **This action cannot be undone.** Confirm **Cancel reservation**. The app refunds the deposit on cancel (no separate **Refund** button).
- No pagination called out. No bulk. No export/print.

## Done when
After refresh the reservation is **Active**, or after convert the person is on **Tenants** and the room is occupied.

## Watch out for
**Reservation deposit required.** Convert needs **Lease Template**. Cancel cannot be undone. This is not **Add Tenant** — convert when they actually move in.
