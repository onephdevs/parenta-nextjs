# Job: Collect rent (record a payment against an invoice)

**Replaces:** GCash screenshot in Messenger or chat, then a tick on the Excel ledger.

**Who does this:** Office admin / Owner

**When:** Every time a tenant pays (GCash, Maya, bank, cash, or cheque).

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Payments** (`/admin/financial/payments`). Click **Process Payment**. Choose **Property**, optional **Room**, then **Tenant**. Click **Continue**.
2. Under **1. Select invoice to pay**, click the invoice row (rent, deposit, or utilities). Under **2. Enter payment details** fill **Mode of Payment** (GCash, Maya, Bank transfer, Cash, Cheque, Other), **Payment Date**, and **Amount Received**. Optional: **OR Date**, **Notes**, **Upload Proof of Payment** (**Take photo** or **Choose file**).
3. Click **Confirm Payment**, then **Yes, Confirm Payment**.

## Done when
You land on the payment receipt (`/admin/financial/payments/[id]`). After refresh, **Payments** shows **Paid** (or **Partially paid**) for that invoice. To undo a recorded payment, open that receipt: **Refund** keeps the row as Refunded (tenant can pay again — new payment id); **Void** removes a mistake.

## Watch out for
**Confirm Payment** stays off until an invoice is selected and **Amount Received** is greater than 0. If you see **No unpaid invoices for this tenant**, there is nothing to apply the payment to yet.
