# Job: Name a unit group for shared bills

**Replaces:** Remembering “3rd floor shares one water meter” in a notebook.

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** Before splitting a bill that is not the whole building (e.g. Balibago 3rd-floor water).

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Bills & Expenses** → **Unit groups** (`/admin/bills-expenses/unit-groups`).
2. **New group:** **Building**, **Name** (e.g. Balibago 3rd floor water), **Utility type** (**Water**, **Electricity**, **Any / unspecified**). Tick member rooms. Optional **Add floor {n}**.
3. Click **Create group**. On **Add Bill**, choose that group under **Unit group (preferred)**.

## Also on this page
- Filter **All buildings**. **Edit group** / **Update group**. **Deactivate** (immediate — no confirm; hides from the active list).
- **Cancel**. **Members ({n})**. **Description**.
- No search. No sort. No pagination. No export. No hard Delete.
- Empty: **No unit groups yet**.

## Done when
After refresh the group is under **Active groups**. Equal-split **Add Bill** can select it.

## Watch out for
**Building and name are required.** **Deactivate** does not ask for confirmation.
