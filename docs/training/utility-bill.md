# Job: Split a utility bill across rooms

**Replaces:** Calculator + Excel split of one Meralco or water bill across occupied rooms (vacant share absorbed by owner).

**Who does this:** Office admin

**When:** A building-wide electric or water bill arrives.

## Steps
1. Sidebar **Bills & Expenses** → **Utility Bills** (`/admin/bills-expenses/utility-bills`). Click **Add Bill**.
2. **Location:** set **Scope** to **Building-wide (common area)** and choose **Building**. **Utility:** set **Utility Type** (**Electric** or **Water**) and **Allocation method** to **Shared — manual split**. Check **Equal-split across units (creates a per-room share; vacant rooms are owner-absorbed)**. Optional: **Unit group (preferred)** or **Floor filter**. **Period:** **Billing Period Start**, **Billing Period End**, **Due Date**. **Amount:** enter **Amount**.
3. Click **Create Bill**.

## Done when
The bill appears on **Room Utility Bills**. After refresh, each included room has its share; vacant rooms are marked owner-absorbed.

## Watch out for
**Amount must be greater than 0.** Building-wide bills need **Building**. If **Scope** is **Specific unit / room**, you must pick **Unit / Room** — that path does not split.
